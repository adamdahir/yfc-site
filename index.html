What we believe

Yakima Foursquare is rooted in the four-fold gospel of Jesus Christ — Savior, Baptizer, Healer, and Coming King. We hold to the full authority of Scripture and the ongoing work of the Holy Spirit. We are part of the International Church of the Foursquare Gospel, a movement of over 90,000 churches worldwide.

("Foursquare" refers to these four beliefs — it has no connection to the social app.)

What We Believe
01

Jesus — Savior

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God who died for our sins and rose again. Through faith in him, every person can receive forgiveness and new life.

02

Jesus — Baptizer in the Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit, which empowers believers for witness, ministry, and a deeper life with God. At YFC, you'll experience Spirit-led worship, prayer for healing, and space for the Holy Spirit to move — all grounded in Scripture. We welcome spiritual gifts and believe the Spirit is as active today as in the early church.

03

Jesus — Healer

We believe Jesus heals today — body, mind, and spirit. We pray for healing and trust in God's grace and perfect timing.

04

Jesus — Coming King

We believe in the literal, physical return of Jesus Christ to establish his kingdom. This hope shapes how we live now.

05

The Authority of Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God — the final standard for faith and practice. Every teaching at YFC is tested against Scripture. We read it, preach from it, and live by it.

06

Water Baptism & Communion

We practice baptism in water by immersion as a public declaration of faith in Jesus. We also celebrate Communion regularly as a remembrance of Christ's death and resurrection — both are open to all who believe.

Full Statement of Faith

The International Church of the Foursquare Gospel maintains a complete statement of faith covering all major doctrines — the Trinity, salvation, Scripture, the church, and more.

    Belong

    Faith was never
    meant to be solo.

    Life Groups are small communities of 8–14 people meeting throughout the week — in homes, coffee shops, and around tables. They're where attendance becomes belonging and belonging becomes transformation.

    Groups meet around various schedules and interests. Some study Scripture together. Some are organized around life stage — young adults, young families, men, women. Cheree will help you find the right fit.

    Find My Group

    How it works

    Talk to Cheree. She'll ask a few questions — where you live, what your schedule looks like, what you're looking for — and connect you with the right group. Most people are in a group within two weeks.

    What we study

    Most groups follow along with the Sunday sermon series — so what you hear on Sunday, you discuss on Wednesday. Some groups use stand-alone Bible study materials. Either way, you'll leave fed.

    Want to lead one?

    Leading a Life Group is Stage 3 and Stage 4 at the same time — you're developing as a leader and sending others deeper into community. If you feel the pull, talk to Cheree.

    Membership

    Membership is
    saying it out loud.

    Belonging to a church isn't a feeling — it's a commitment you make and one the church makes back to you. Membership names that in both directions: you commit to this body, and we commit to shepherd, know, and care for you.

    Acts 2:42 · They devoted themselves.

    What membership means here

    • —You understand and affirm what we believe
    • —You gather consistently and belong to a Life Group
    • —You give and receive care, prayer, and accountability
    • —You have pastors who know your name

    Equipping and transforming students into effective leaders by integrating Life Pacific University’s biblical education with real-life ministry at Yakima Foursquare Church — growing in character, calling, and community impact.

    Apply Compare the two tracks

    Yakima Foursquare Church signed its micro campus agreement with Life Pacific University in March 2026.

    Two tracks

    Which one is you?

    The difference is not the degree — both tracks earn the same accredited LPU degrees. The difference is how much of your week the church holds.

    Traditional

    Ages 18–25, in a formative season, able to commit to a structured week.

    Immersive, hands-on training alongside your degree. You are expected to be teachable, spiritually hungry, and open to broad ministry exposure as you discern calling and grow in character and competence.

    • Structured weekly schedule
    • Full internship rotations
    • Intentional spiritual formation
    • Active participation in the life of the church

    Flex

    Adult learners balancing work, family, or existing ministry responsibilities.

    Greater scheduling flexibility and focused engagement, so you can grow academically and spiritually without full internship immersion. You may serve in ministry areas aligned with your experience and calling.

    The progression

    Exposure, then ownership,
    then multiplication.

    Year 1

    Exploration & Formation

    Broad ministry exposure across multiple environments rather than early specialisation, so you understand how a church actually works.

    • Spiritual formation
    • Ministry exposure
    • Character development
    • Learning how the church functions

    Year 2

    Apprenticeship & Ownership

    Focused, in-depth training inside one ministry area. You move from exposure to ownership and become a key contributor.

    • Specialised ministry training
    • Leadership responsibility
    • Mentorship
    • Skill refinement

    Years 3 & 4

    Leadership & Multiplication

    Flexible but intentional options as callings and capacities diverge, holding the same standard for leadership readiness and character.

    • Leading others
    • Mentoring
    • Strategic ministry leadership
    • Discipline

    If that shape looks familiar, it should — it is the same movement as the church’s own pathway: Begin, Belong, Contribute, Multiply. See the pathway

    What it costs,
    plainly.

    Tuition

    Set by LPU

    Micro campus tuition is charged at a reduced rate compared with studying on the residential campus. Billed by Life Pacific University, not the church.

    Our site fee

    $2,000per year

    Paid to the church, on top of LPU tuition. This covers running the campus here — the mentorship, the ministry placement, and the people who do it.

    Most students qualify

    Handled by LPU’s financial aid office. Worth applying before you rule anything out on cost.
